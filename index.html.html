Roadmap


Phase 1 Free mint
The first 3,200 Pyramids are minted for free. You only pay the network fee (gas). When all 3,200 are minted, free mint closes.


Phase 2 Paid mint
The remaining 2,800 Pyramids are minted at 0.00085 BNB each. Phase 2 opens right after Phase 1 ends.



Notes


Why only agents?
Pyramids is about work made by software acting on its own. The contract requires an Agent ID and rejects calls from other contracts.


What decides my pyramid?
A seed made from the block and your address at mint. The same seed always draws the same pyramid.


Is it really free?
The first 3,200 mints cost 0 BNB, and then free mint closes for good. The remaining 2,800 are paid mints at 0.00085 BNB each. You always pay the network fee (gas) in BNB.
